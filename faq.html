Frequently Asked Questions

What services does MRA provide?

Plain concrete, coloured concrete, exposed aggregate concrete, slate concrete, stencil concrete, resealing / acid washing, garage epoxys, bobcat works, foundations / slabs, council crossovers / footpaths, concrete retaining walls, site levels and turf installation.

What is included with the concrete?

All concrete includes F62 mesh, crushed rock base and a 100mm thick concrete minimum. Expansion foam is provided around all brickwork and cut after concrete has settled in-lieu with brickwork.

Does MRA handle council works?

We also offer council works including crossovers & footpaths. We make the hassle extremely easy when dealing with the council and deal directly with them to ensure works are hassle free for the owner.

What is included with exposed aggregate concrete?

Exposed aggregate concrete includes multiple small demo cuts, acid washing & sealing.

Can MRA provide custom exposed aggregate mixes?

We are able to provide custom exposed aggregate mixes suitable to the client.

Why can exposed aggregate colour vary?

There may be minor variations in the consistency of colours, textures and appearance of the finished concrete as the basic raw materials are natural and subject to variations beyond our control. Customers should always view the samples.
